Rules, sources and what this page does not claim

Simple squeeze

Fixed ranking: relative strength, sector momentum, compression and EMA34 proximity. Same squeeze opportunity pool and risk/exit engine as payoff.

Payoff squeeze

Frozen boosted model estimates realized ATR payoff, using price, volume, nesting and historical earnings information. A score is not a win probability.

Tight Flag Breakout

Strong thrust, short tight flag, breakout, RS admission percentile ≥70 and SPY above 200-day. Entry must meet both extension and stop-distance limits.

Squeeze management: structural/two-ATR initial stop; three-entry-ATR trailing stop after a two-ATR advance; maximum 63 sessions. Both models’ paper choices use the frozen global top-three selector, sector constraint and ticker cooldown—not the older top-three-per-family display.

Combining the page does not merge mandates, double size for agreement or rewrite the historical selection rules. No orders, option prices or broker positions are implied. Any displayed provider earnings date is a schedule, not independently confirmed. Current analyst annotations expire when the market session changes.
